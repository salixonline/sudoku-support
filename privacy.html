Privacy policy

Draft prepared October 24, 2026 · App package: com.lux.sudoku

Draft for review. This policy describes the current ad-free app source, version 0.1.20. Before publication, confirm the final release behavior, confirm hosting practices, and set an effective date.

Who provides the app

Sudoku is provided by Salix. Privacy questions can be sent to salixonline@gmail.com.

Information the app keeps locally

The app stores puzzle progress, entered numbers and notes, game history needed for undo and redo, elapsed time, completion statistics, daily challenge progress, streaks, theme and input settings, and pause-game scores and sound preferences. This information supports gameplay and remembers your preferences in the app’s private storage on your device.

Collection and sharing

The current app does not transmit gameplay information or personal information to the developer or third parties. It has no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud synchronization. Local statistics are calculated on your device. The app does not sell your information.

Permissions and device services

The current app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier. It does not request Android’s internet permission. Android, your device manufacturer, and Google Play provide their own services under their respective privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and statistics remain until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Saved games can be replaced as you play; the previous daily game is replaced when a new daily challenge is prepared. To remove all locally stored app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device). This resets saved games, settings, and statistics and cannot be undone through the app.

The app disables Android application backup in its configuration and does not provide cloud recovery. Device transfer or backup behavior may still depend on Android and the device manufacturer.

If you contact support

If you choose to email support, your email provider and the support mailbox provider process the message. The developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and investigate the issue. Do not include passwords or unrelated sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting the support address above.

This website and external services

These pages do not use analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or remote fonts. This copy is a local preview. When a public version is hosted, the hosting provider may process standard connection information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. The final policy will identify that provider and link its privacy information before publication.

Children

The current app does not ask for age or date of birth and does not transmit personal information from players. Please avoid sending children’s personal information in support messages.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s practices change. If advertising, analytics, accounts, or online features are introduced, their data handling will be described before that version is released. The published policy will display its effective date.
