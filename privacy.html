Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Last updated October 27, 2026 · com.lux.sudoku

Who provides the app

Sudoku (com.lux.sudoku) is provided by Salix. For privacy questions or support, email salixonline@gmail.com.

Information kept on your device

The app keeps regular and daily puzzle progress, entered numbers, pencil notes, selected cells, undo/redo history, elapsed time, pause/completion state, statistics, and daily streaks. It also stores the local date, hints used that day, and bonus hints earned from puzzle completion to calculate your daily hint allowance on the device. It also remembers your chosen theme, board appearance, number-input and wheel-menu preferences, language choice (system, English, or Spanish), whether the quick guide has been shown, and sound, vibration, animation, and other gameplay settings. The pause mini-game keeps a local best score and sound preference.

These records use the app’s private device storage to resume games, calculate local statistics, and remember your preferences. Legacy local coin balances or reward records may remain from earlier versions; earning coins is currently disabled and no purchase system is active.

Theme packs and chosen files

The app includes theme packs and lets you manually import a theme archive through Android’s document picker. It reads the file you select, validates the archive, and copies its theme metadata, images, colors, and optional sound files into private app storage. Theme files are used for appearance and sound; they are not executable plug-ins. Installing or updating a theme replaces that theme’s installed copy without deleting your saved puzzles or statistics.

The app does not automatically download themes. A file provider you choose in Android’s picker may obtain files from its own cloud service under its own privacy practices; that is separate from a download service operated by Sudoku. The app does not request broad access to your files.

If you export a theme pack, the export contains that theme’s files, not your game saves, statistics, or settings. Android’s document picker determines the destination, which may be local or a cloud provider you choose. Clearing Sudoku’s storage does not delete the original imported archive or any exported copy outside the app; manage those separately with the file provider.

Collection and sharing

The current app does not automatically send gameplay data or personal information to Salix or an analytics server. There is no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, billing SDK, or developer-operated cloud synchronization. Puzzle generation and statistics run on your device. The app does not sell your information. User-chosen file operations, support email, and external websites are described separately below.

Permissions and external services

The current app declares no Android permissions, including INTERNET and the advertising-ID permission. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Manual import/export uses Android’s document picker to access a chosen file or destination. Android, your file provider, your device manufacturer, and Google Play operate their own services under their own privacy policies.

Privacy-reference links open outside the policy text when you tap them; support opens your email app. These actions do not automatically attach gameplay records or diagnostics. The public-policy shortcut opens the hosted policy in your browser when you choose it. An optional Play Store promotion destination is currently unset, so that destination is unavailable.

Retention, removal, and deletion

Private settings, statistics, imported themes, and legacy records remain until replaced, removed where supported, or the app’s storage is cleared. Regular saved games can be replaced while playing; the previous daily save is removed when a new daily challenge is prepared. The current theme settings do not expose a general theme-removal control. Included theme packs are installed or restored from bundled assets at startup.

To remove all private app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies). This removes private installed themes, saved games, statistics, language/guide preferences, and other settings. It cannot be undone through the app. When you launch again, included themes and default preferences are created again. Uninstalling normally removes private app storage too. Neither operation deletes support emails or original/imported/exported files kept outside the app.

There is no online account to delete and no developer-operated cloud recovery. Android application backup is disabled in the manifest; device backup or transfer behavior can still depend on Android and the manufacturer.

Support email

Tapping Contact support opens an email composer addressed to salixonline@gmail.com; you decide whether to send it. The app does not automatically send a message or attach diagnostic logs, screenshots, or game records. If you send a message, Salix receives your address, message, and any attachments you choose. Your email provider and Gmail, which hosts the support mailbox, process the correspondence under their own policies.

Support information is used to respond to requests and investigate issues, and is retained only as necessary to handle the request and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion at the same address. Avoid sending passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Website hosting

The local website and bundled policy do not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, or remote fonts. The public support website is hosted on GitHub Pages at salixonline.github.io/sudoku-support/.

When you visit a version hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes even without signing in. Hosting-related processing is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection information. Loading the bundled offline text alone does not contact GitHub. Opening a link uses your browser and the destination’s privacy practices.

Intended audience and children

Sudoku is intended for teens and adults aged 13 and over. This is the intended audience, not an age-verification feature.

The current app does not ask for age or date of birth and does not automatically transmit personal information from players. Avoid including children’s personal information in support messages.

Planned features and policy changes

Advertising, in-app purchases, and automatic theme downloads are planned possibilities, not current functionality. This policy does not describe them as enabled or authorize future collection in advance. Before releasing features that change data handling, Salix will update this policy, relevant store disclosures, and any required notices or consent controls. The last-updated date will change when the policy is revised.
