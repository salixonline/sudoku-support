Does Sudoku need an account or connection?

The current version works offline and does not require an account. Games, preferences, and statistics are saved locally.

Can I move my progress to another device?

The app does not currently offer account-based synchronization, cloud recovery, or a manual export feature. Keep your current installation and its storage if you want to preserve progress.

How do I change the appearance?

Open the app’s settings to choose a theme and adjust board appearance and input preferences. Imported theme packs are copied into private app storage. There is currently no general theme-removal control in settings.

How do I delete my data?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Sudoku → Storage → Clear storage. Device wording may vary. This removes saved games, preferences (including language and guide status), statistics, and private imported theme copies; the app cannot recover them afterward. Included themes are recreated at the next launch. Original theme archives and exported files outside the app are not deleted. There is no online account to delete.

Report a problem

Email salixonline@gmail.com with the app version, Android version, device model, steps to reproduce the issue, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot can help; crop out personal information first. Sending diagnostics is optional and manual.
